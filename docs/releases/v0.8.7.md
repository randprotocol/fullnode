# v0.8.7 — chain 22 on constraint set 11 (release notes)

Written 2026-11-20 on `release/v0.8.7`, from fullnode main `e79070a7`. **Nothing here has been
tagged, signed, published or run against a host.** The owner tags and signs on a real TTY. The
cut, `stop` (the halt of chain 21) and every fleet phase run only on the owner's go, typed in the
executing session, one phase at a time.

v0.8.7 is **the chain-22 release, a chain cut**. It is never rolled onto chain 21. A set-11
`rand-node` refuses every chain 14–21 genesis by chain id (`constraint_set::build_genesis`) before
it touches a datadir. So the fleet switches from v0.8.6 to v0.8.7 in one all-stop cutover
(`deploy/cutover-fleet-chain22.sh`), and a host cannot run both chains on one binary. Chain 21's
datadirs are never touched, and chain 21 stays the rollback target on v0.8.6.

The row-by-row list is the `CHANGELOG.md` entry for v0.8.7. The full record (pins, measured runs,
traps) is `AGENTS.md`. This page covers the four things an operator or a reviewer needs from the
release:

1. the fee change, as a governance record;
2. the chain-22 cut;
3. the upgrade steps;
4. the rollback.

**What a user pays.** At the default gas price of 100 units per gas, the gas term of a bundle's
fee goes from 2 047 900 units (0.0020479 RAND) to 2 252 700 units (0.0022527 RAND). That is
+204 800 units, about 0.0002 RAND per bundle. The auth proof's ceiling moves by +12 800 units.
The msauth ceiling falls by 8 038 400 units (about 0.008 RAND). A call's fee is still priced at
the full-height poseidon2 ceiling, which over-charges a proof that declares a shorter table and
never under-charges one. Wallets declare the ceilings by default, so no user action is needed
beyond a chain-22 wallet build. Issue #167 stays open for what set 11 does not address.

## 2. The chain-22 cut

The full field list, the owner's decisions and the checks are in `docs/deploy.md`, "The next cut:
chain 22". In brief:

- **Constraint set 11.** Grain-derived Poseidon2 round constants. Every guest hc, note
  commitment, nullifier, Merkle and state root and verifier key moves. Proofs also declare their
  poseidon2 height, use the rate-¼ quotient layout (FRI blowup 2, 92 queries, 24 pow bits) and
  versioned rVM keys. Guests: bundle v3 `00782b22…141e`, auth `8373994c…6078`, msauth
  `41db73c5…9cd1`. The production msauth key blob is `168d79a8…71b3` (23 851 bytes).
- **EVM block context** (circuits `41d64acd`, issue #168). Under `InvokeAt` (wire tag 45), the
  EVM guest's `NUMBER`, `TIMESTAMP`, `CHAINID`, `BASEFEE`, `GASPRICE` and `PREVRANDAO` read
  public words 11–27. `program_state.reference_block_window` is 256.
- **The randomness beacon.** Hash-chain commit–reveal (`BeaconReveal` tag 46, `BeaconCommit` tag
  47) with `commit_delay_blocks` 256. There is one commitment per validator, made on its own
  host. Total seed loss halts the chain for good. The owner accepted that risk on 2026-11-18, to
  be covered by each operator's offline encrypted backup.
- **Perps.** BTC-PERP on zUSD, tier 18, 24 inputs, as on chain 21. The engine image keeps the
  same bytes, now at the set-11 hc `482215a8…5073`, and the set-11 `genesis_root` is
  `a45d8fda…98b4`. `perps.state_proof_keys` pins four tier-18 shapes (CON-601), and validators
  load those keys instead of building them. Chain 21's exchange is carried, and it is empty.
- **The treasury.** 5 of 6 with six new signers. Its 100 000 RAND is carried from chain 21's
  `multisig_rand_held`, not issued again.
- **Validators.** 25 (18 genesis + 7 bonded), quorum 17. archive2 is dropped and its 1 000 RAND
  chain-21 stake is not recreated (named on the record's `drops:` line).
- **History.** Chain 21's history on record starts at height 187 556 (e, obs1). The carry scan
  runs with `PRUNED_SCAN_OK=1` and the record's `scan-floor: 187556`.
- **Program vaults are dropped.** durian.market's AMM is redeployed on chain 22 with a fresh
  pool.
- **Other genesis-gated rules switched on at the cut:**
  - `program_state.refuse_panics` (PST-1);
  - `perps.bound_payouts` (PERP-6);
  - `perps.sparse_windows` with `max_window_heights` 256 (PERP-101);
  - `perps.max_pending_digests` 65 536 (PERP-5);
  - `multisig.max_accounts` 65 536 (MSA-4);
  - `tokens.incremental_root`.
- **Chain-22 genesis hash:** `PLACEHOLDER-CHAIN22-GENESIS-HASH`.

## 3. Upgrade steps

### a. The tag, the release, the signature

The owner runs these; the exact sequence is in the v0.8.7 release PR. In outline:

1. Tag `v0.8.7` (annotated, naming the green `ci` run) on fullnode main.
2. `release.yml` builds and attests the release. It publishes `rand-node`, `rand`,
   `rand-prover`, `msauth-vk-production.bin`, the four `perp-vk-production-t18-*.bin` files and
   `SHA256SUMS`.
3. The owner signs `SHA256SUMS` off the build host with `deploy/sign-release.sh` and uploads
   `SHA256SUMS.sig`.
4. Verify all of the following before any host is touched:
   - the signature, with `verify_release_sums`;
   - each file against the signed sums, with `verify_binary`;
   - each file's attestation, with `gh attestation verify`;
   - the msauth blob is `168d79a8…71b3` and 23 851 bytes;
   - `rand-node keygen-perp-vk --print-pinned production` names the four sha256s in
     `docs/deploy.md`.

`SHA256SUMS` has eight lines. Seven of them are the files `cutover-fleet-chain22.sh` stages and
re-checks on every host: `rand-node`, `rand`, `msauth-vk-production.bin` and the four perps keys.
The eighth is `rand-prover`, which the cutover does not stage (the prover hosts take it
separately). The cutover refuses a
signed file that does not list all seven at their decided values.

### b. The fleet (all 25 hosts): `deploy/cutover-fleet-chain22.sh`

Run each phase **alone**, never piped or chained, and read `echo $?` before the next (the chain-16
trap). `INVENTORY=…/configuration/fleet/inventory.tsv` is needed by every phase.
`RELEASE_SUMS` is the signed v0.8.7 `SHA256SUMS`, `OLD_RELEASE_SUMS` v0.8.6's (chain 21: rand-node
`61c2a8bb…`, msauth blob `68facb06…cd30`). Chain 21 keeps committing until `stop`.

1. `preflight` (read-only): all 25 hosts reachable on v0.8.6 and chain 21, disk, keys, and the
   signed v0.8.7 sums and files.
2. `stage`: the seven files and `check-beacon-seed.sh` go to `/root/stage-c22` on every host,
   sha-checked.
3. `beacon-init`: each validator makes **its own** seed with the staged v0.8.7 binary. **Each
   validator operator** then keeps an offline, encrypted backup of `beacon-c22/beacon.seed.json`.
   The seed never leaves its host.
4. `beacon-collect` (read-only): the 25 commitments are written to `BEACON_TSV`.
5. `bridge-stop`: rand-guardian on the six guardian hosts, and rand-relayer on `RELAYER_HOST`.
6. `snapshot <dir>` and `balances <dir>`, against a tunnel to e or obs1 (`CHAIN21_RPC`), with
   chain 21's v0.8.6 `rand` as `WALLET`.
7. `cut`, with these inputs:
   - `EXPECT_NODE_VERSION=0.8.7`;
   - `PERPS_MAX_WINDOW_HEIGHTS=256`;
   - `MULTISIG_MAX_ACCOUNTS=65536`;
   - `TREASURY=~/.rand-chain22/treasury.txt`;
   - `PROGRAM_VAULTS_DROPPED_OK=1`, `PRUNED_SCAN_OK=1`;
   - the collected `BEACON_TSV`.

   The cut writes `deploy/genesis-chain22.json` and the cut record, and prints the genesis hash
   and the `constraint-set-row:`. **→ fill `PLACEHOLDER-CHAIN22-GENESIS-HASH`.**
8. The second operator re-derives the hash and writes `second-hash:` into the record.
9. **Commit** `deploy/genesis-chain22.json`, the cut record, and the row
   `(22, "<hash>", 11),` in `crates/randprotocol-node/src/constraint_set.rs`. Raise the array
   length to 9. In the same commit, replace this page's placeholders. Then run
   `cargo test -p randprotocol-node --lib constraint_set` on the build droplet, never on the Mac.
10. `push <genesis>`: the genesis goes to every host. Chain 21 is **still running**.
11. **The owner's go**, typed in the executing session.
12. `CONFIRM_HALT_CHAIN21=halt-chain-21-for-<first 8 hex of the hash> stop <genesis>`. This stops
    all 25 hosts and halts chain 21.
13. `switch <genesis>`: the v0.8.6 files become `*.pre-c22`, then the v0.8.7 files are installed,
    the new datadirs created (marker `11`) and each seed copied into its host's own datadir.
14. `start`: bootstraps C and D first, then the other 21 validators.
15. `check-limits <genesis>`: the cut script's read-back against C, run **before the first deposit
    and state proof**.
16. `start-archives`: e and obs1.
17. `wait <genesis>`: all 25 on chain 22, keys loaded, `check-beacon-seed.sh --rpc` passing.
18. `BRIDGE_ON_CHAIN22=yes bridge-start`, once the bridge session has switched the daemons'
    configs (chain id, cursor).
19. `verify <genesis>`: the `CHAIN_CONSTRAINT_SETS` row and the roll record
    (`deploy/roll-records/v0.8.7-chain22-cutover.record`).

`SELFTEST=1 deploy/cutover-fleet-chain22.sh` rehearses all of this against 26 fake hosts, and
`DRY_RUN=1` prints every remote command.

### c. A node outside the fleet (an RPC node, an archive, a developer's node)

1. Install v0.8.7's `rand-node` and `rand` and, **beside the binary**, `msauth-vk-production.bin`
   (set 11) and the four `perp-vk-production-t18-*.bin`. Without them a chain-22 node refuses to
   start. To keep them elsewhere, use `--msauth-vk` / `RAND_MSAUTH_VK` and `--perps-key-dir` /
   `RAND_PERPS_KEY_DIR`.
2. Make a **new** datadir: `rand-node init --datadir <new dir> --genesis genesis-chain22.json`.
   This writes `constraint-set` = `11`. Copy `constraint-set` with `genesis.json` and `db/` if a
   datadir is ever moved. Do not point v0.8.7 at a chain-21 datadir: it is refused, and left
   byte-identical.
3. A validator only: create the seed with `rand-node beacon init --datadir <dir> --key <node key>
   --chain-id 22` on that host, before the cut. Back it up offline and encrypted, and never copy
   it to another host. Before the first start, run `deploy/check-beacon-seed.sh --genesis
   genesis-chain22.json --datadir <dir> --key <node key>`.
4. Start the node.
   - Check `rand_getVersion` reports 0.8.7.
   - Check `rand_status.msauth_key` and `rand_status.perps_keys` report the keys loaded.
   - Check `rand_getBeacon` is `enabled`.

### d. Downstream, at the cut (each in its own repository)

- **Wallets.** Wallets need a set-11 core: clients `2f80ad5e` (#14) is chain-22-only. A wallet
  that still proves for set 9 cannot transact on chain 22. Mobile still has to migrate a saved
  chain 21 to 22.
- **randscan, durian.market, randbridge.org, randprotocol.org.** Each moves its chain pin and
  expected genesis to chain 22.
- **The bridge.** The relayer and guardians mint against set-11 commitments on chain 22.
- **clients CI.** `FULLNODE_TAG` becomes `v0.8.7` only after the tag exists.

## 4. Rollback

Rollback is possible **only while chain 22 has burned and minted nothing**. Once a bridge burn,
deposit or mint has committed on chain 22, going back loses it. From then on, problems are fixed
forward. Stop the bridge first (`bridge-stop`).

1. `quiet <genesis>` (read-only): confirms nothing was burned or minted on chain 22.
2. `halt`: stops a fleet that does not run chain 21. It refuses a fleet that does.
3. `OLD_RELEASE_SUMS=<v0.8.6 SHA256SUMS> rollback`: restores chain 21's units,
   `/root/rand-node.pre-c22`, `/root/rand.pre-c22` and
   `/root/msauth-vk-production.bin.pre-c22`. v0.8.6 reads chain 21's set-9 blob, so restoring it
   is required. The four perps key files may stay, because chain 21's genesis pins none.
4. `start`: the rollback marker licenses it, with C and D first.
5. `wait21`: waits until all 25 serve chain 21 and C's height moves. Chain 21 resumes from the
   height where it stopped, because its datadirs were never touched.

**By hand**, if `rollback` itself cannot run:

- restore the three `*.pre-c22` files into `/usr/local/bin`;
- on a node host, restore `/root/rand-node.service.<old>.bak` over the unit;
- on a guardian host, move `/root/chain21.conf.c21` back into
  `/etc/systemd/system/rand-node.service.d/` and delete `chain22.conf`;
- then `daemon-reload` and start.

**A re-cut after a switch** runs `halt`, `rollback`, `start` and `wait21`, then `push`, `stop` and
`switch` on the new genesis. The beacon seeds stay in each host's staging dir and are reused, so
`BEACON_TSV` is unchanged. Each re-cut has a new genesis hash, so the `CHAIN_CONSTRAINT_SETS` row
and this page's placeholders follow it.

**Not a rollback.** Never install v0.8.6 over a chain-22 datadir, or v0.8.7 over a chain-21 one.
Each build refuses the other's datadir by its marker and writes nothing.
